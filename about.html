Our Story

More Than a Meal.

The story of Delish — where Nigerian cooking meets contemporary dining, in the heart of Port Harcourt.

Why Delish Exists

Built around a single idea: respect for the food.

Delish was founded on the belief that Port Harcourt deserved a restaurant where the food you grew up loving is treated with the same discipline as the world's great cuisines — where jollof is slow-cooked with intent, and suya is treated like a fine cut.

Our kitchen draws global inspiration but keeps its feet firmly in local soil: fresh produce from Rivers State markets, spices and herbs chosen by hand, and recipes that honour the flavours Nigerians grew up with.

Hospitality is the other half of our promise. Every guest should feel genuinely welcome — whether it is a first date, a family Sunday, or a business dinner that needs to impress.
